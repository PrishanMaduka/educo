The Quad logo

A quad is the courtyard at the heart of a campus, where everyone crosses paths. The mark draws that courtyard from above: four groups around one shared centre, with a tail that turns the ring into a Q.

How the mark is built

One ring, 9 units thick on a 64-unit grid, cut into four equal arcs at the diagonals.

Four arcsThe school, its teachers, parents and students. They're separate, but they make one circle.
The centre dotThe quad itself: the shared space where they all meet, and the one warm colour in the mark.
The tailLeaves through a gap on the 45° line, so the ring reads as a Q and points outward.

Wordmark

Custom lowercase letters drawn with the same 9-unit stroke as the mark. The q, a and d share one bowl, so the word looks as even as the four arcs. The letters are drawn as shapes, so they look the same everywhere with no font installed.

App icon

The white mark on a blue-to-teal gradient, for the parent app, browser tabs and the home-screen shortcut for the admin app. Use the icon file below 24 px, where the full-colour arcs become hard to tell apart.

Using the logo

✓Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the letters
✓Use the white version on navy, blue or photos
✕Don’t stretch or squash it
✕Don’t recolour the arcs
✕Don’t rotate it. The tail must stay at the bottom right
✕Don’t put the colour logo on blue
