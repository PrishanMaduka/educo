The Quad brand

A quad is the courtyard at the heart of a campus, where everyone crosses paths. The mark is four soft petals around a shared centre: the teachers, the family, the people who care for a child, and the child. Navy, cream and four bright accents, Bricolage Grotesque headings and flat, friendly people carry it from the public site into every app.

How the mark is built

Four petals on a 30-unit grid. Each is a 13.5-unit square with a 10-unit round outer corner and 3.5-unit inner corners, mirrored into the four quarters with a 3-unit gap, so the centre stays open.

Wordmark

Lowercase “quad” in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, tightly spaced and outlined as shapes, so it looks the same everywhere with no font installed. Navy #101632 on light backgrounds and cream #F7F5F0 on dark ones. The petals keep their colours on both.

Colour

Navy and cream do most of the work. The four accents are for categories, people and illustrations, and lime and pink mark the two audiences: lime for schools, pink for parents. Status colours (green, orange, red, blue) and every other value are in the app design system.

The accents always carry navy text. As text on cream or white they are too light, so the apps use their deeper -ink versions.

Type

Two families from Google Fonts, self-hosted in the apps.

Display and headingsBricolage

Bricolage Grotesque, weights 600–800 with optical sizing. Greetings, page and card titles, big numbers and the wordmark. Tight tracking (−0.02 to −0.045em).

Body and interfaceFigtree

Figtree, weights 400–800. Everything people read and fill in: paragraphs, tables, forms, labels and buttons. Tabular figures in tables.

App icon

The colour mark on navy, for the parent app (one Quad app for every school), browser tabs and the staff portal’s home-screen shortcut. The petals stay clear down to 16 px. Development and staging builds add a small “DEV” or “STG” badge.

Schools’ own colours

Each school picks one colour in the console. Inside its staff portal and in the Quad parent app after sign-in, that colour replaces Quad’s on buttons, the active item and selected things. Quad’s navy, the petals and the type stay. A school that hasn’t picked a colour gets Quad lime.

  1. Text on a school-coloured button is white when the colour can carry it, otherwise navy. The colour is then darkened or lightened only as far as needed for 4.5:1.
  2. Links, icons and selected text use a deeper or lighter version of the colour that reads 4.5:1 on every surface.
  3. In dark mode a deep colour is lifted so buttons still stand out from the card.
  4. The side bar stays Quad navy for every school; its active item is the school’s colour, lifted to stand out from navy.
  5. Focus rings are always blue, so they never disappear into a school’s colour.
  6. Before a school is known (the parent app’s welcome and sign-in, the staff sign-in), screens are Quad-branded: navy, petals and pink for parents or lime for schools.

Using the logo

Leave clear space at least the height of the petals
Use the white wordmark on navy, dark colours or photos
Don’t stretch or squash it
Don’t recolour the petals, even in a school’s colour
Don’t rotate it. Sky always sits top left
Don’t put the colour logo on an accent colour; use the white mark
