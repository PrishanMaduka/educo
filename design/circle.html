A concept for Quad

Every child has a circle

Schools run on modules: attendance, marks, fees. Families don't. They want to know how their child's day went and who is looking after them. Quad Circle puts the child at the centre and everyone who cares for them around it. Teachers share moments in seconds, the school day shows up for parents as it happens, and the system looks after the relationship between the two.

Four ideas behind the circle

Each one answers something parents and teachers told us goes wrong with school systems today: silence until there is a problem, messages at 10 pm, and reports that arrive too late to help.

The day, as it happens

The parent app opens on a ring of today's timetable. Arrival, registration, lessons, lunch and the bus home fill in on their own from data the school already records. Parents see where their child is without asking.

Moments, not messages

Teachers share a moment in under ten seconds: tap the children, pick praise, learning, photo or heads-up, and it reaches only those families. No group chats, no reply-all.

  • Templates write the first draft with each child's name
  • Skill tags build a picture of progress over the term

Everyone around the child

Parents see the people in their child's circle by name and role, what each is working on with the child, and when they reply. Saying thank you is one tap, and it goes to the teacher, not into a void.

Good relationships by design

A family pulse shows each teacher which families haven't heard anything positive lately, and nudges before a heads-up goes out. Quiet hours hold messages overnight so teachers get their evenings back.

For the school: is every family connected?

Leaders get one view of how well the school is talking with families. It shows which classes are sharing positives and which families haven't been reached. It isn't a league table of teachers.

How it lands in Quad

The circle reuses what Quad already has: timetables, registers, teaching assignments, reports and branding. It changes what each person sees first.

Parent app

Opens on the child's day

  • Home becomes the day ring and a feed of moments
  • A Circle tab lists teachers with office hours and a thank-you button
  • Learning tab: this week's topics with one thing to try at home
  • Fees, forms and the bus stay one tap away
Teachers

Capture and care

  • My teaching gets a Capture button and class roster
  • Family pulse with positives, heads-ups and replies per family
  • Quiet hours and reply-time expectations shared with parents
  • Moments feed report comments at the end of term
School

Connection, not surveillance

  • Connection view by section and class
  • Families not reached get a gentle follow-up list
  • Photo consent and who-sees-what rules per child
  • Translation of moments into Sinhala and Tamil
