A school system that feels like your school

Quad runs the whole school, from admissions to fees to report cards, and it is built for schools like ours. Every screen opens with what is happening and what needs doing, in plain words, before any tables or charts.

What makes Quad different

!Early warning

Spots students who have started slipping in attendance, behaviour, learning or homework, says why, and suggests who should act. One click starts a support plan. The console does the same for schools.

✦Ask Quad

Ask in plain English: “Who needs attention in Year 9?”, “What do I owe?”, “Draft a letter about the trip”. Answers come from the school’s own records and show their sources.

♥Quad Circle

Every child has a circle. Teachers share moments and one thing to try at home; parents see the day as it happens, who looks after their child, and can invite grandparents to see moments.
