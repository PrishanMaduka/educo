Design prototypes · sample school: Greenfield International

Every child has a circle.

Quad runs the whole school, from admissions to fees, and keeps everyone who looks after a child close: the teacher who notices, the coach who cheers, the nurse who checks in and the family at home. Every screen opens with what is happening and what needs doing, in plain words, before any tables or charts.

What makes Quad different

Quad Circle

Teachers share moments and one thing to try at home. Parents see the day as it happens, who looks after their child, and can invite grandparents to see moments.

Early warning

Spots students who have started slipping in attendance, behaviour, learning or homework, says why, and suggests who should act. The console does the same for schools.

Ask Quad

Ask in plain English: “Who needs attention in Year 9?”, “What do I owe?”, “Draft a letter about the trip”. Answers come from the school’s own records and show their sources.

The apps

For school staff

Staff portal

Today opens with a story of the school day and what needs you, then early warning with support plans, Moments, family connection, admissions, pastoral care, timetable, cover, exams, fees and settings. Forms open in drawers on the right.

For families

Parent app

A Quad-branded welcome, then the school’s own colours: Maya’s day as it happens, the Circle tab with moments, people and learning, gentle heads-ups, Ask Quad, payments, the bus, bookings and forms. One login for every child.

Every school at a glance with its own colour, early warning for schools that might leave, plans and billing, curricula, branding, users and roles. Quad lime marks the console’s own actions.
