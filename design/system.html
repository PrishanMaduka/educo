Quad app design system · staff, console and parent

The landing page’s warmth, tuned for a working day.

Navy, cream and four bright accents, Bricolage headings and flat people, used calmly: cream pages, white cards, colour only where it means something (status, categories and the school’s own colour), stories before tables, and forms in right-side drawers.

Calm canvas

Cream page, white cards, navy bar

The page is quiet so the work stands out. Dashed dividers inside cards, soft shadows, 20 px corners and pill buttons.

Colour means something

Status, category or the school

Green, orange, red and blue are only for status. Lime, pink, sky and orange tag categories and people. The school colour marks actions and where you are.

Two typefaces

Bricolage speaks, Figtree works

Bricolage Grotesque for greetings, page titles, card titles and big numbers. Figtree for everything you read and fill in: tables, forms, labels.

Colour

Every colour is a token on :root in design/system/tokens.css, with a dark value. The values below are read live from the page, so they follow the theme and the school colour you pick.

Foundations

The school’s colour, always readable

A school picks one colour in the console. Inside its staff portal and parent app it replaces the brand tokens. system/brand.js derives the rest so text passes WCAG AA for any colour, in light and dark. Pick a school to re-theme this whole page.

The named colours a school can pick in the console (QuadBrand.PALETTE), Quad lime first as the default. Any other colour works too.

Selected 12 A link in the brand colour
  1. Buttons and badges use brand-fill with brand-ink. The ink is white when the colour can carry it (3:1 or more), otherwise navy. The fill then moves away from the ink, 1% at a time, until the text is 4.5:1.
  2. Dark mode first lifts a deep colour toward white until it stands out 3:1 from the card, so a maroon or navy school still has a visible button.
  3. Text, links and icons use brand-text: the colour moved toward black (light) or white (dark) until it is 4.5:1 on the canvas, cards, table headers and brand-soft.
  4. The side bar stays Quad navy for every school. Its active item uses rail-active, lifted until it stands out 3:1 from the bar.
  5. Focus rings are always focus blue, so they never vanish into a school’s colour.

Buttons and fields

Buttons say what happens. One primary action per view; the rest are secondary or ghost. Fields have a visible label, a 3:1 border and errors in words.

Buttons

On navy (story cards, the side bar) the primary button uses rail-active, which is lifted to stand out from navy.

Buttons and chips are named by their label, so a line-strong outline is enough. Inputs, checkboxes, radios and switches show their edge or state with field-line and switch-off, 3:1 on cards in both themes.

Rules that change in spec 03

  • “Indigo side bar, coral actions, lilac highlights” becomes navy side bar, school-colour actions, lime highlights.
  • The side bar is no longer tinted by the school colour (rail, rail-2 are fixed navy).
  • The console’s active item is Quad lime, not violet.
  • brand-ink is chosen per colour (white or navy), not always white.
  • New brand-text for brand-coloured text and icons; never put text in brand directly.
  • Focus rings use focus blue instead of the brand.
  • The default school colour (no colour chosen) is Quad lime #C8F169. Schools that saved #DD4A42 keep it.
  • Heat steps: pink for none, lime for a little to a lot (were coral and teal).
  • line-strong is decorative. Inputs, checkboxes, radios and switches show their edge or state with field-line and switch-off, 3:1 on cards.
