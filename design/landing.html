The Quad Circle

What is the Quad Circle?

The Circle is the small group of people at school and at home who care for one child, and the simple ways Quad keeps them in touch.

How the circle works

  1. 1A moment at schoolTwo taps, and only Amaya’s family sees her mural.
  2. 2Home says thanksMum taps thank you. Grandma, invited, loves it.
  3. 3Try it at homeOne small idea from class. They tap We tried it.
  4. 4The teacher sees itHer family pulse shows who needs good news next.

Every week, round again

What keeps it kind and safe

  • Families choose who’s inParents invite up to four relatives. Relatives only see moments, and can be removed in one tap.
  • Photos only with permissionParents choose who sees photos of their child: the class, family only, or no photos.
  • Quiet hoursTeachers aren’t pinged from 18:00 to 07:00 or at weekends. Urgent safety alerts still go through.
  • Good news, not recordsHealth and safeguarding notes never go into the Circle, and relatives never see fees, marks or attendance.
A day in the circle

One school day in Amaya’s circle

School systems are good at records and bad at relationships. Parents hear nothing until something goes wrong, and teachers get messages at 10 pm. Circle uses what a school already records to keep families close. Here is a Monday for Amaya, Year 4 Emerald.

  1. 07:42
    The day, as it happens

    Amaya’s mum sees she’s arrived

    The gate scan and the register start a ring of Amaya’s day on her mum’s phone. Lessons, lunch and the bus home appear as they happen. Nobody had to send a message.

  2. 08:55
    Moments

    Her teacher shares a moment in ten seconds

    Ms. Jayasinghe taps Amaya’s name, picks Photo, writes a line and tags the skill she noticed. Only Amaya’s family sees it, and only if the family has agreed to photos.

  3. 09:10
    Family circle

    Grandma in Kandy loves it too

    Parents can invite up to four relatives to see moments. Grandparents never see fees, results, attendance or health, and can be removed with one tap.

  4. 12:30
    Everyone around the child

    Dad sees who looks after Amaya

    Every teacher, the coach and the nurse, with what each is working on with Amaya and when they reply. Saying thank you is one tap, and it reaches the teacher.

  5. 18:20
    This week in class

    They try one thing at home

    Each week teachers share what the class is learning and one small thing to try at home. Amaya and her mum write a postcard to Grandma, tap We tried it, and Ms. Jayasinghe sees it the next morning.

  6. 19:30
    Quiet hours

    Teachers get their evenings back

    A parent can still write at 19:30. Quad tells them kindly that it reaches the teacher at 07:00, and holds the teacher’s notifications until morning. Urgent safety alerts always go through.

  7. Fri
    Weekly recap

    A week with Amaya, in one card

    The moments, the skills her teachers noticed, days in school, what the family tried at home, and one idea for the weekend.

Families choose who sees photos of their child. Nothing about health or safeguarding ever goes into Circle.
